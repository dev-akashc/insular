Insular is a FLOSS fork of Island, maintained by Akash.
With Insular, you can:<ul>
<li>Isolate your Big Brother apps (e.g., Facebook, TikTok, etc.)</li>
<li>Clone and run multiple accounts simultaneously for apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, and more</li>
<li>Freeze or archive apps to block all background activities</li>
<li>Unfreeze apps on-demand using home screen shortcuts</li>
<li>Re-freeze selected apps with a single tap</li>
<li>Hide apps completely from your main profile</li>
<li>Selectively enable or disable VPN for different sets of apps</li>
<li>Prohibit USB access to mitigate physical access attacks</li>
<li>Run dual app environments (personal/work) with full separation</li>
<li>Manage app permissions separately across profiles</li>
<li>Disable system apps without root in the work profile</li>
</ul>

If your device is incompatible or not encrypted, you can skip this limitation manually. Please refer to <a href="https://forum.xda-developers.com/android/-t3366295">the XDA post</a> for details.
To uninstall and remove Insular completely, first "Destroy Insular" from **Settings → Setup** by clicking the recycle-bin icon beside Insular. If you already uninstalled the Insular app, go to your device **Settings → Accounts** and manually "Remove work profile".

<b>PERMISSIONS</b><ul>
<li>DEVICE-ADMIN: Required to create the Insular space (work profile), which is fundamental to how Insular operates. It will be explicitly requested for your consent.</li>
<li>PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Required to detect and manage the running state of apps. It will also be requested explicitly.</li>
</ul>

We never collect any user data, and Insular does **not** request or use Internet permissions. Please read our privacy policy for more details.

<b>Differences from Island</b><ul>
<li>All proprietary blobs (Google Play services, Crashlytics, analytics, etc.) are removed to comply with F-Droid’s policy</li>
<li>Internet access of this app is removed—because it simply isn't needed</li>
<li>Fully reproducible and open-source—auditable by anyone</li>
<li>All changes and future updates are maintained by Akash</li>
</ul>
